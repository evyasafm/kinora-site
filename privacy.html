Kinora Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026 · Evyasaf Mordechai

Summary

Kinora edits your photos and videos on your device. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. The only time a photo leaves your device is when you choose to use an AI feature.

What stays on your device

  • Photos and videos you add to a project, and the projects themselves.
  • Audio you import, and the beat analysis of it.
  • Videos you export. They are saved to your Photos library only when you tap Save to Photos.

None of this is uploaded to us. Deleting a project or the app removes it.

AI features

When you choose to generate an AI transition or AI scene, the photos for that generation are sent through our server to two providers that create the video: fal.ai (video generation) and Anthropic (which looks at the photos to describe the camera movement). The photos are used only to produce your video. Our server passes them along and does not keep them. The providers process them under their own privacy policies and may retain them for a limited time.

Do not use AI features with photos of other people without their permission.

Data our server stores

  • A random app identifier created on your device, used only to keep your AI credit balance. It is not linked to your name, email or Apple Account.
  • Your AI credit balance and the identifiers of credit purchases, so each purchase is credited once.
  • Your IP address, briefly, to prevent abuse of free credits.

Purchases

Subscriptions and credit packs are processed by Apple. We receive Apple's signed confirmation of a purchase so we can provide what you bought. We never see your name, payment card or Apple Account email.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect contact details, location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or analytics about how you use the app, and we do not sell or share data with advertisers.

Children

Kinora is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: contact support.
