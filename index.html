Kinora

Beat-synced reel maker for iPhone

Drop in photos and clips, pick a template, and Kinora cuts every shot to the music. Add transitions, looks and text, then export for Reels, TikTok and Shorts.

Support

Questions, problems or feedback? Contact support and we'll get back to you.

  • Subscriptions are managed in your Apple Account: Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Restore purchases from Kinora → Settings → Restore purchases.
  • AI credits: one credit makes one AI transition or scene. If a generation fails, the credit is returned automatically.
